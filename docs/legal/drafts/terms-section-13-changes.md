# Terms of Service §13 "Changes" — DRAFT replacement text

> **Draft for the owner (FX-44). Not published, not reviewed by a lawyer.**
> The owner asked for the Terms to say it is the user's responsibility to review
> changes. The published §13 instead promises at least 30 days' notice of a
> change that matters. This draft keeps that promise — withdrawing a notice
> period people relied on would itself be a change that leaves them worse off —
> and adds the responsibility the owner asked for beside it.

## Proposed text

## 13. Changes

We may change these terms. We will not change them quietly.

**How we tell you.** When a change matters, we tell you in the app at least
**30 days** before it takes effect, and by email if you have not turned those
off. The notice links to the new version and shows what changed.

**What we ask of you.** When we tell you about a change, it is your
responsibility to read it. If you are under 18, we ask a parent or guardian to
read it with you.

**Two kinds of change.**

- A **clarification** — clearer wording, a fixed typo, a new example — gets a new
  minor version and the notice above. If you keep using WeVolunteer after it
  takes effect, the clarified terms apply to you.
- A change that **leaves you worse off** — new information collected or shared,
  a right taken away, information kept longer — gets a new major version, and we
  ask you to accept it before you carry on. Until you do, you can still sign in,
  read it, download your information and close your account; you cannot take
  part in new volunteering until you accept.

**If you do not agree,** you can close your account at any time, before or after
a change takes effect, and download your information first. Your verified record
stays verifiable for anybody you gave a certificate code to, as the Privacy
Policy explains.

**Earlier versions.** Every version is kept with a content fingerprint, so a copy
you were given can be checked against the one your account accepted. Email
**legal@wevolunteers.org** for any previous version and the dates it applied.

## Also in the same version: §4.2

Replace "we are one person running a volunteering platform for a school
district, not a company with a bounty programme" with **"WeVolunteer is run by
one person, not a company with a bounty programme"**. Under Posture B the
platform is not run for a district.

## Notes for the owner

- **[OWNER] Check against the product before publishing.** Each sentence above
  describes what the software does today: a minor version raises a banner and
  sends an email; a major version on the Terms opens a dialog that must be
  answered and withholds participation until it is, from the version's
  effective date; the editor refuses an effective date sooner than 30 days for a
  major change. If any of that changes, this text must change with it.
- **[OWNER] This is a major version.** It adds a responsibility, so it asks every
  account to accept again — including the student whose acceptance currently
  blocks the Privacy Policy repair. Consider publishing it together with that
  repair and the Acceptable Use Policy, so people are asked once.
- **[OWNER] "Continuing to use means you accept"** is only used for
  clarifications here, deliberately. For minors, a court may not hold a child to
  terms accepted by silence, which is why anything that leaves a reader worse off
  asks for an explicit yes.
