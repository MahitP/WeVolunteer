# WeVolunteer Student User Guide

Welcome to WeVolunteer! This guide will help you navigate the platform to find volunteer opportunities, manage your applications, and track your hours.

## Getting Started

### Account Creation & Login
1. Go to the WeVolunteer homepage and click **Sign Up**.
2. Select **Student** as your role.
3. Fill out your basic information (Name, Email, Password, Grade).
4. After signing up, you can log in at any time from the homepage.

### Email Verification
To ensure you can receive important updates about events (like schedule changes or acceptances), you must verify your email.
- Check your inbox for a verification email after signing up.
- Click the link to verify your account.

## Finding and Joining Events

### Browsing Events
- The main **Dashboard** will show you upcoming and open events.
- You can filter events by category (e.g., Environment, Library, Tutoring) to find opportunities that interest you.
- Click on any event card to view the description, location, application deadline, and available volunteer slots.

### Applying to Volunteer
1. On the event details page, review the available slots and their times.
2. Choose the slot that fits your schedule and click **Apply**.
3. Some events accept volunteers automatically (Auto Select), while others require the Organizer to review applications.

### Understanding Selection Status
Once you apply, your application will be in one of the following states:
- **Pending:** The organizer is reviewing your application.
- **Accepted:** You are confirmed for the shift! Look for instructions on where to go.
- **Waitlisted:** The event is full, but you are in line if someone drops out.
- **Withdrawn:** You have cancelled your application.

### Withdrawing Applications
If you realize you can no longer attend an event, please withdraw your application as soon as possible so another student can take your spot!
1. Go to your **Profile** or **Dashboard**.
2. Find the application and click **Withdraw**.
3. **Note:** If the event has already happened, or is actively in progress, you cannot withdraw.

## Tracking Your Impact

### Viewing Volunteer Hours
- Navigate to your **Profile**.
- You will see a summary of your Total Credited Hours.
- Hours are credited after you attend an event and the Organizer logs your attendance. 

### Downloading Certificates
- Once hours are credited for an event, you can download an official Certificate of Volunteering.
- Go to your Profile, find the completed event under your past events, and click **Download Certificate**. This PDF can be printed or emailed directly to your school counselors!

## Managing Your Account

### Profile Settings
You can personalize your WeVolunteer experience:
- Click **Edit Profile** to change your Avatar, write a short Bio, or list your Skills (e.g., Cooking, Cleaning, Setup).
- You can also opt-in or opt-out of the public Leaderboard.

### Logging Out on Mobile
If you are using WeVolunteer on a shared phone or tablet:
- Tap the **Sign Out** button located directly on the bottom navigation bar to securely log out.
