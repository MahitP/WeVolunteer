# WeVolunteer Organizer User Guide

Thank you for organizing community service opportunities! This guide explains how to use WeVolunteer to create events, manage students, and securely award hours.

## Getting Started

When you sign up as an Organizer, your account will be **Pending** until a District Administrator approves it. Once approved, you have full access to create events.

## Creating and Managing Events

### Creating Events
1. Log in and go to your **Organizer Dashboard**.
2. Click **Create Event**.
3. Fill out the details: Title, Description, Category, Location, and Application Deadline.
4. **Visibility:** You can choose who sees your event's specific instructions. By default, only 'accepted' applicants see the specific meeting instructions (to protect privacy).
5. Add **Slots** (shifts or roles). For example, "Box Packer" from 9 AM to 12 PM for 5 students.

### Auto Select vs. Manual Review
When creating an event, you can enable **Auto Select**:
- **Auto Select ON:** Students are automatically accepted the moment they apply, up until the maximum slot capacity is reached. Additional applicants are waitlisted.
- **Auto Select OFF:** You must manually review each applicant and accept them.

## Managing Applicants

### Accepting/Rejecting Volunteers
If Auto Select is off:
1. Go to your Event Dashboard and click on the **Applicants** tab.
2. Review the list of pending students.
3. You can click **Accept** or **Reject** individually, or use the **Bulk Actions** checkbox to accept multiple students at once!

### Managing Capacity and Waitlists
- WeVolunteer strictly prevents you from accepting more students than your slot capacity allows.
- If a student withdraws, their spot opens up, and you can accept someone from the waitlist.

### Communicating Instructions
Any text you place in the "Instructions" field of your event is securely displayed only to the appropriate students. Ensure you mention where they should meet, who to ask for, and what to wear.

## Tracking Attendance and Hours

### Taking Attendance
1. During or immediately after your event, go to the **Check-in** tab on your event page.
2. You will see a roster of all *Accepted* volunteers.
3. Mark students as Present.

### Crediting Hours
1. Once the event has ended, go to the **Closeout** tab.
2. Review the final attendance list.
3. Click **Award Hours** to finalize the roster.
4. Once every shift has ended and every confirmed volunteer has been accounted
   for, the event is marked **Complete**. That protects it from accidental
   deletion and releases students' official hour certificates. **It does not
   freeze the hours** — see Closing Out below.

### Correcting Attendance Records
If you make a mistake (e.g., someone left early and earned 1 hour instead of 2):
1. Go to the **Closeout** tab.
2. Change the mark or the number, and give a reason for the change.
3. Changes automatically update the student's dashboard and regenerate their certificate.

You can do this at any time before you close the hours out, including after the
event is marked Complete. Every change is recorded against your name.

### Closing Out the Hours
When the numbers are right, press **Close out hours** on the Closeout tab.

- It is only offered once the event is Complete, so nobody can be left
  uncredited by it.
- You will be asked to confirm, and told how many volunteers it affects.
- **It cannot be undone.** After this, nobody can change those hours — not you,
  and not an administrator.

That permanence is the point: it is what makes a WeVolunteer certificate worth
quoting to a scholarship committee. Take the extra minute to check the hours
first — there is no rush, and an event left un-closed stays correctable.

Your dashboard lists finished events whose hours are still open, under **"Ready
when you are"**. It is a reminder, not a warning: nothing is wrong with an event
sitting there, and the hours are credited and visible to students either way.

> **Two different "close" actions.** *Close sign-ups* stops new applications
> arriving and can be reversed. *Close out hours* permanently locks credited
> service hours and cannot.

### Viewing Volunteer History
Your Organizer Dashboard gives you a complete historical view of all events you have hosted and the students who helped make them possible.
